How to get a Gmail App Password:
1. Enable 2-Step Verification on your Google account
2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
3. Create an app password (16 chars) — paste it in the password field above
4. This is NOT your regular Gmail password — it's a special code for apps

Get a free key at web3forms.com. A copy of each notification is delivered to your inbox — forward it to the recipient.

⚠️ EmailJS Template Setup (IMPORTANT):
Your template at emailjs.com must use these exact variables:
• To Email: {{to_email}} (required — this is the recipient)
• Subject: {{subject}}
• From Name: {{from_name}}
• Content: {{{message}}} (TRIPLE braces — renders HTML email body)
If your Content field shows [html] or {{message}} (double braces), change it to {{{message}}} (triple braces) or emails will arrive blank!

ℹ️ About

SOS WALLETS v2 — Admin-Only Mode

This is a private control panel for managing your crypto wallet operations, recipient notifications, and email delivery. No public signups — you log in as admin and control everything from here. All data is stored server-side in a SQLite database and persists across devices and sessions.
