How the email reaches the recipient:
  • You enter the recipient's email in the "Recipient email" field in the app
  • After sending, an email opens pre-filled with the subject and message below
  • Tap "Send Email Notification Now" → it opens their mail app with everything filled in
  • The recipient gets the email shown below in their inbox
How the webhook reaches the recipient:
  • You enter a webhook URL (e.g. a Discord/Slack/Telegram bot endpoint, or your own server)
  • The app sends a POST request with the JSON payload below to that URL
  • The recipient's system receives it and can display it however they want (Discord message, push notification, dashboard alert, etc.)
